              Platting questions

              Straight answers about plats in Texas

              What is a replat?

              A replat redraws all or part of a recorded plat, for example to combine two lots into one or split a lot into two. It replaces only the portion being replatted and is approved and recorded like any plat.

              How long does a plat take in Dallas?

              Dallas accepts plat applications on published Wednesday and Thursday submittal dates, and the City Plan Commission hears them about four weeks later. Survey and drawing come before that; conditions, signatures and county recording come after. The owner paperwork is what most often sets the pace, which is why the checklist above starts on day one.

              Do I need a plat to get a building permit?

              Many Texas cities, Dallas included, issue building permits only on a platted lot of record. Land described by metes and bounds or as an acreage tract often needs a plat first.

              What is the difference between a preliminary and a final plat?

              A preliminary plat lays out lots, streets and utilities so the engineering plans can be designed and approved. The final plat is the document that is signed and recorded at the county once those conditions are met.

              How long does a Texas city have to act on a plat?

              Under Local Government Code Chapter 212, as amended in 2023, a city must approve, approve with conditions, or disapprove a complete plat application within 30 days of filing. If conditions are given, the applicant can respond and the city has 15 days to act on the response.
